Controls & cold survival

WASD / arrows move; mouse looks; Shift runs; hold C to crouch. E opens inventory. F enters or leaves nearby shelter. Esc pauses.

The timer shows approximate active outdoor seconds until death. Cold exposure: warning at 120, urgent at 240, death at 360 active game seconds. Outdoors adds 1 per second. Bare tents hold exposure steady; an indoor sleeping bag restores 3 per second; a fox den restores 1 per second. Outdoor bags give no warmth. Pause and spectator mode freeze exposure.

Use E → Tent → Place, then approach the zipper and press F. Setup, positioning and entry take time: remain exposed until inside. Place a sleeping bag from inside a tent to recover warmth. Placement is permanent for this expedition. Click places, R rotates, Esc cancels. Choose dry, clear, gently sloping ground or clear indoor floor away from walls and the doorway.
